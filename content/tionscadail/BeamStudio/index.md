An interactive tool for learning structural engineering, where you adjust a beam's supports and
loading and watch the shear force and bending moment diagrams recompute in real time as you drag. It
also has a gamified 'Bridge Challenge' mode, which sets design problems and scores your solution, so
the underlying mechanics are learned by experimenting rather than by reading them off a page.
